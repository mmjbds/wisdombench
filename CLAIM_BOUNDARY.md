# Claim Boundary

WisdomBench measures longitudinal behavior under the tasks, prompts, rounds, scoring rules, judges, model versions, and seeds included in this artifact.

The public release supports inspection of task definitions, score computation, aggregate result files, and the included sensitivity analyses. It does not establish:

- General wisdom or general intelligence.
- General or production safety.
- A causal effect outside the evaluated strategies and conditions.
- A population-level negative relationship from the reported `rho = -0.389`, `p = 0.212`, `n = 12` exploratory correlation.
- Customer, deployment, clinical, financial, or real-world performance.

RFR is conditional on the benchmark's severe-threshold failure definition and available denominator. Compare task-level records and metric definitions before interpreting a zero or low value.

This repository remains anonymized for double-blind review. Identity or homepage cross-links must not be introduced while that requirement is active.
