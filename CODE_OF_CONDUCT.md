# Code of Conduct

Be rigorous, direct, and specific. Critique claims, methods, code, data, and artifacts; do not attack people.

Reproduction failures, negative results, boundary corrections, and disagreement are welcome when they identify the affected version, evidence, and a path another person can inspect. Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publication of private or identifying material are not accepted.

Conduct reports that cannot be public should use the private route documented at https://github.com/mmjbds/mianzhang.org/blob/main/.github/CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md.
